interior angle - angle inside a shape
exterior angle - angle outside of a shape formed by extending a side
sum of exterior angles will always be equal to 360 degrees for all polygons

sum of interior angles: $(n - 2) * 180$ where n is the number of sides. 
It works because each polygon can be divided into triangles. For example, a square has 4 angles which must be 90 degrees. If we place two triangles inside, then because the sum of the angles in each triangle must be 180, we can prove that $(n - 2) * 180$ will be equal to 4 angles of 90 degrees

the angle between two points in a circle is defined as $\theta = s/r$ where s is the arc length  and r is the radius

**pi** = ratio between a circle's perimeter (circumference) and its diameter $\pi = C/d$ or $\pi = C/2r | *2$ 
$2\pi = C/r$

radians = a radian is an angle measurement. A radian is defined by the arc whose length is equal to the radius of the circle (for example, 2rad = 2 radii of the circle). If the arc is equal to the circumference, then the angle will be $2\pi rad$ because if we use the formula to determine the angle between two points and the circumference formula, then
$C = 2\pi r$
$s = \theta *r$ | $2\pi r = \theta * r$ | $2\pi = \theta$ 
from this formula, we can determine the angle of a single radian. We know that $\theta$ of a full circle is 360, so $2\pi x = 360$ | x = $360 / 2\pi = 57.29\ degrees$
