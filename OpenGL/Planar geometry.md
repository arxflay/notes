**Circle circumreference** is line around circle (defining the whole circle). Computed via $2\pi r = C$ (r is repeated $2 \pi$ to form circle )

**Arc length**: Computed via $S = \theta  r$, where $\theta$ is angle in radian. Formula is derived from ratio of arc length and ratio of rotation, $S/C = \theta / 2\pi$, $S/2\pi r= \theta / 2\pi$, $S = \theta  r$, proved by substition S = C, then $\theta$ must be $2\pi$, thus $1 = 1$, If angle is not in radian, then formula contains conversion from degree to rad $S = \dfrac{\theta \pi r}{180}$

**Circle area**: formula is $A = \dfrac{1}{2} 2\pi r^2 = \pi r^2$, which can be imagined as $r^2$ half of the square repeated $\pi$ times.

**Circle sector** is a closed region formed inside circle from center and two points on circle (disk). Typical types are half disk($180 \textdegree$), quadrant ($90 \textdegree$) sextant ($60\textdegree$)
Area of circle sector is derived from formula of circle area, $A = \dfrac{\theta r^2}{2}$ (proved by picking $\theta$ equal to $2 \pi$)
## Chord intersection theorem

^00d4f6

If two chord intersect and divide chord in two length: a and b for first chord and c and d for second chord, then $ab = cd$, e.g area of two rectangle that formed by divided chords are identical. The reason is that intersected chords form 2 triangles in point of intersection. Each triangle is similar triangle, because both angles are on same arc [[Angles#^8de587 |Subtended angle theorem]].
