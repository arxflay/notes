## Chord intersection theorem

^00d4f6

If two chord intersect and divide chord in two length: a and b for first chord and c and d for second chord, then $ab = cd$, e.g area of two rectangle that formed by divided chords are identical. The reason is that intersected chords form 2 triangles in point of intersection. Each triangle is similar triangle, because both angles are on same arc [[Angles#^8de587 |Subtended angle theorem]].
